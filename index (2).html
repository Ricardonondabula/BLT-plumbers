Not sure you have a leak? Test it in 30 minutes.

You only need your water meter. Run this test before you call, and tell us what you find.

  1. Make sure no water is used anywhere on the property for 30 minutes.
  2. Write down the number on your water meter at the start.
  3. After 30 minutes, check the meter again. If the number has changed, water is escaping. Contact us.
